Automatic Plant Watering System – Components List

1. Arduino UNO
2. Soil Moisture Sensor (Analog)
3. 5V Relay Module
4. Submersible Water Pump / DC Water Pump
5. Jumper Wires (Male-to-Male / Male-to-Female)
6. Breadboard
7. External Power Supply (5V/9V depending on pump)
8. USB Cable for Arduino
9. Connecting Tube (optional)
10. LED Indicator (optional)
